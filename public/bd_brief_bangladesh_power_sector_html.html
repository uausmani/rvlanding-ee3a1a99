The Burning Platform: System Context

This section outlines the macroeconomic and structural drivers forcing the energy transition in Bangladesh. The sector is currently characterized by severe fossil-fuel lock-in, extreme overcapacity, and mounting financial distress for state utilities. Understanding this data is critical for positioning our advisory services not just as environmental compliance, but as existential operational and financial resilience.

90-Day BD Execution Plan

A pragmatic roadmap to establish our presence, leverage field visits, and secure initial bridgehead diagnostic projects that convert into long-term advisory mandates.

1

Days 1–30: Intelligence & "Wedges"

  • Identify 5–8 target IPPs facing imminent DFI refinancing or compliance pressure.
  • Develop brief "teasers" focusing on pain points: Grid curtailment, ESG data gaps, Asset stranding.
  • Formalize local partnerships (e.g., local engineering firms, universities) to augment our boutique footprint.
  • Wedge Offerings: Quick ESG maturity diagnostic, High-level BESS integration study.
2

Days 30–60: Pilot Conversations & Diagnostics

  • Leverage current field contacts visiting plants: Equip them with 1-pagers specific to Gas, Coal, or RE assets.
  • Propose "no-regrets" 2-4 week rapid assessments (loss reduction or E&S gap analysis) at modest/success-based fees.
  • Engage DFIs (ADB, World Bank) as an agile execution partner for their local Technical Assistance (TA) grants.
3

Days 60–90: Conversion & Flagship Setup

  • Convert pilot diagnostic findings into multi-month structured programs (e.g., 12-month ESG buildout, MACC roadmap).
  • Secure one "Flagship" engagement (e.g., ESG architecture for a marquee IPP or BPDB entity) for future credentialing.
  • Convene a private roundtable: Connect a progressive Distribution Utility with RMG Exporters to workshop Corporate PPAs.
